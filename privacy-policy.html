1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data only with your explicit consent:

  • Personal identifiers: name, age, gender, address, government ID number
  • Financial information: self-declared household income, monthly expenses, existing loan details, bank or UPI transaction history (if uploaded)
  • Digital activity: UPI transaction count, mobile recharge frequency (entered manually — we do not access your device directly)
  • Device metadata: browser type, screen size (for app functionality only)
We never collect your Aadhaar biometrics, bank passwords, OTPs, or any data without a specific consent checkbox.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Verifying basic eligibility (age, income, KYC)
  • Running our AI credit scoring model to predict repayment ability
  • Displaying your result and score breakdown
  • Improving model accuracy using anonymised, aggregated patterns

We do NOT use your data for:

  • Marketing or advertising
  • Selling to third parties
  • Any purpose beyond loan eligibility assessment

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Stored data is AES-256 encrypted at rest
  • Raw documents (bank statements, ID uploads) are deleted within 24 hours of processing
  • Derived features (expense ratio, score) are retained for 90 days then permanently deleted
  • We use role-based access — only the scoring system can read your data; no human staff has access to individual records
This prototype stores data in localStorage only (your browser). No data leaves your device in this version.

4. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to fix inaccurate information
  • Erasure: Request complete deletion of your data at any time
  • Grievance: Raise a complaint with our Data Protection Officer
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke any consent given, stopping all processing

To exercise any right, email: privacy@graamcredit.in
We will respond within 7 business days.

5. Cookies

GraamCredit uses only essential cookies:

  • Session cookie: keeps you logged in during your application
  • Preference cookie: remembers your language choice (EN/हिंदी)

We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. No third-party cookies are loaded on this site.

6. Third Parties

We do not share your data with any third party. This prototype does not integrate with Account Aggregators, payment gateways, or any external APIs. All processing happens client-side.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via a notice on the homepage at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of GraamCredit after that date constitutes acceptance.
