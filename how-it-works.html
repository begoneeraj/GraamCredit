HOW IT WORKS

From application to approval — in 5 minutes

A transparent, step-by-step process built for rural India. No CIBIL score. No branch visits.

1
STEP 1

Create your profile

Enter your full name, age, and gender. Upload a government-issued ID — Aadhaar card, Voter ID, or NREGA job card. This takes under 60 seconds and is the only document we need from you.

2
STEP 2

Share your financial details

Tell us your household's annual income, monthly expenses, and any existing loan EMIs. You can also upload a bank or UPI statement (CSV format) for a more accurate score — this is optional but recommended.

3
STEP 3

AI checks your eligibility

Our model evaluates 10+ factors: expense ratio, UPI transaction frequency, debt load, mobile recharge history, and more. No CIBIL score is needed. The entire analysis happens in seconds, not days.

4
STEP 4

Get your result instantly

Your result screen shows your credit score out of 100, a breakdown of every eligibility rule, and your maximum loan amount if approved. If rejected, you'll see exactly why and how to improve.

Common questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is encrypted using HTTPS. We store only the derived features needed for scoring — raw documents and statements are deleted immediately after processing. We follow RBI and India's DPDP Act 2023.

Do I need a CIBIL score to apply?

No. GraamCredit uses alternative data sources — UPI history, mobile activity, income-expense ratio — specifically because most rural borrowers don't have a formal credit history.

What loan amount can I get?

Based on RBI microfinance guidelines, loans are available up to ₹3 lakh for households with annual income under ₹3 lakh. Your exact amount depends on your credit score and debt ratio.
