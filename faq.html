Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GraamCredit and microloan eligibility

Who can apply for a microloan through GraamCredit?
Any Indian resident who meets all of the following: is 18 years or older, has a household annual income of ₹3 lakh or less, does not have an existing active microloan, has total monthly debt obligations below 50% of income, and can provide a valid government ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or NREGA card).
Can salaried employees apply?
GraamCredit is designed specifically for low-income rural households — farmers, daily labourers, and small business owners. Formal sector employees with regular salaries typically have access to bank loans and are outside the target segment. However, the eligibility checker will still assess your profile based on income thresholds.
What is the maximum loan amount I can get?
As per RBI Master Directions on Microfinance (2022), eligible households can receive collateral-free microloans. The exact amount depends on your credit score, income, and debt ratio. GraamCredit estimates eligibility up to ₹50,000 for first-time borrowers with strong scores.
Can I apply if I already have a personal loan from a bank?
A personal bank loan is different from a microloan. The restriction is specifically on active microfinance loans (from MFIs or NBFCs under RBI microfinance guidelines). A bank personal loan counts toward your debt ratio calculation but does not automatically disqualify you.
What if I'm rejected — can I apply again?
Yes. GraamCredit shows you exactly why you were rejected and what to improve. For example, if your debt ratio is too high, paying down an EMI and reapplying in 3 months could change your result.
Is there an age limit on the upper end?
There is no strict upper age limit in our model. However, many MFIs operationally prefer borrowers under 65. Our system will flag this as a soft warning but not a hard rejection.
Does GraamCredit store my Aadhaar number?
In this prototype version, all data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and never transmitted to any server. Your Aadhaar or Voter ID number is used only for KYC verification display and is not sent anywhere.
Can I delete my data after applying?
Yes. In the result page, there is a 'Delete My Data' button that clears all localStorage entries associated with your application. This is permanent and immediate.
Do you sell data to banks or NBFCs?
No. GraamCredit does not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party — financial institution, advertiser, or otherwise. This is both a legal commitment and a technical fact: no external API calls are made in this prototype.
Is this DPDP Act 2023 compliant?
GraamCredit is designed with DPDP Act 2023 principles: explicit consent before each data collection category, data minimisation (only necessary fields), right to erasure, and purpose limitation. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
What is a credit score in GraamCredit?
It is a number from 0 to 100 that represents your predicted likelihood of repaying a microloan on time. It is calculated by our ML model using 10+ factors including income-to-expense ratio, UPI activity, mobile recharge frequency, debt load, and group membership.
Why don't you use CIBIL scores?
Most rural, low-income borrowers have no formal credit history and therefore no CIBIL score. GraamCredit uses alternative data — UPI usage, mobile activity, income patterns — that better reflect the financial behaviour of this demographic. This is consistent with RBI's push for alternative credit scoring for financial inclusion.
How can I improve my score?
The main factors you can improve: reduce monthly expenses relative to income, pay down existing EMIs to lower your debt ratio, use UPI regularly (higher transaction frequency boosts score), maintain consistent mobile recharges, and join a Self-Help Group (SHG) if possible — group membership adds a repayment track record bonus.
Is the score 100% accurate?
No. GraamCredit is a prototype trained on synthetic data. The score is an estimate based on proxy indicators, not a certified financial assessment. Treat it as a directional guide, not a definitive judgment.
Does GraamCredit actually give out loans?
No. GraamCredit is an eligibility checker and educational prototype only. It does not disburse funds, partner with any MFI or bank, or process real applications. A positive result should be taken to a registered microfinance institution like Bandhan Bank, SKS Microfinance, or government schemes like PM SVANidhi.
What is a microloan exactly?
A microloan (or microfinance loan) is a small, collateral-free loan given to low-income individuals who cannot access formal banking. In India, RBI defines these as loans to households with annual income up to ₹3 lakh, with monthly repayments capped at 50% of income. They are typically used for income-generating activities — small businesses, livestock, agriculture.
What government schemes offer microloans in India?
Key schemes include: PM SVANidhi (street vendors, up to ₹50,000), Mudra Yojana (small business, up to ₹10 lakh), NRLM/DAY-NRLM (rural women via SHGs), and Stand-Up India (SC/ST and women entrepreneurs). GraamCredit eligibility criteria align with PM SVANidhi and NRLM norms.
Does the app work offline?
Partially. The eligibility form and scoring work fully offline since all logic runs in your browser. The page itself needs an initial internet connection to load fonts and icons, but no data is sent to any server.
Which browsers are supported?
GraamCredit works on Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, and Edge 90+. It is optimised for mobile Chrome on Android, which is the primary device of our target users.
Is there a mobile app?
Not yet. GraamCredit is currently a Progressive Web App (PWA) — you can add it to your home screen from Chrome by tapping 'Add to Home Screen' in the browser menu. A physical Android app is planned for a future version.
